The nonthreaded method is better for small n.
In this case, it was faster for n < 100,000,000.
However, the threaded method is better for big n.
In this case, it was faster for n >= 100,000,000.

Graphing the data points above and a power regression
curve onto the graph shows that the nonthreaded method
will require increasingly more time than the threaded method.

The nonthreaded method is probably better for small n because
no time is spent on creating & managing the threads.

The threaded method is probably better for larger n because
it allows the computer to split its resources.
